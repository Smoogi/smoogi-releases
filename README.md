A music player for Android that works fully offline. It plays the music files on your phone, from the folders you choose, and keeps everything it learns about them on the phone: no account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

## Highlights

- **Plays your files:** background playback with lock-screen controls, sleep timer, speed, A-B repeat, fades, and optional skipping of silent intros and outros.
- **Smart Shuffle:** favorites, new songs, forgotten songs and a cooldown, each adjustable, with presets.
- **Queue tabs:** several queues at once, like browser tabs.
- **Your folders only:** the app scans only what you choose; fast search, also in Japanese.
- **Playlists and smart playlists** that fill themselves from rules; add many songs at once; M3U import and export.
- **Lyrics:** from `.lrc` files, word by word when the file has word times, and full screen.
- **Edit details and covers** without touching your files; optional online lookup for single songs or 100 at a time.
- **Statistics:** what you listen to, and how your library divides over genres, artists and albums.
- **Four looks:** Material, Cyberpunk, Frutiger Aero and PC-94, with your own wallpaper.
- **Backup and restore**, also automatic.

Everything in detail: the **[feature guide](FEATURES.md)**.

## Install and update

1. Open the newest release and download `smoogi-music-player-<version>.apk` (the `.sha256` file is only a checksum; you do not install it).
2. Open the downloaded file. Android may ask once to allow installs from your browser; allow it for this install.
3. Open **Smoogi**, choose your music folder(s) and allow access to music files.

Needs Android 10 or newer and a 64-bit ARM phone (almost every phone since 2017).

**Updating:** download the newer `.apk` and open it; it installs over the old version and keeps your library, playlists and settings. Or use **Settings > App updates > Check for updates**, which opens the release page for you. Save a profile first (Settings > Backup & Restore) to be safe.

## Privacy

- No account, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. The app looks nothing up on its own.
- The internet is used only for things you start: an online lookup, lyrics search, or checking for updates (and the optional once-a-day update check, off by default).
- Your music files are only read. Everything the app adds stays in its own storage on the phone.

Full details in the **[privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)**, also in the app under Settings > About.

## Known issues

- Cover art on a car's display over Bluetooth depends on the phone's Bluetooth stack; on at least one recent Samsung update it does not show for any player, not only this one. Settings > Diagnostics > Media session log shows what the app hands to Android.
